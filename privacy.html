Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Mechsim is an open-source mechanics simulator operated by Imtiaz Ahamed. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the Mechsim website and its optional Gemini-powered chat assistant.

Information the simulations use

The simulations run in your browser. You can use the simulator pages without creating an account or providing personal information. Physical parameters and playback controls are used locally to calculate and render the simulations; they are not sent to Mechsim by the simulator itself.

Google connection and Gemini chat

If you choose to connect Google, Mechsim uses Google Identity Services to request an OAuth access token for the Gemini API. Mechsim does not receive your Google password. The access token is kept in the current browser session memory and is not saved in Mechsim cookies or a Mechsim database. Mechsim stores only a non-sensitive local flag indicating that you previously granted access, so it can request a fresh token without showing the consent screen again. You can disconnect and revoke access from the chat window or from your Google Account settings.

When you use the chat assistant, your question, the current conversation messages, the mechanism name, and the mechanism description are sent to the Mechsim Cloudflare Worker. The Worker forwards the request to the Google Gemini API so Gemini can generate an answer. Do not submit passwords, financial information, health information, or other sensitive information to the chat.

Technical information and rate limiting

The chat Worker uses the connecting IP address supplied by Cloudflare to apply a limit of five chat requests per minute per client address. This is used to protect the service from abuse. Cloudflare, Google, and the hosting providers may independently process technical information such as IP addresses, browser requests, and service logs under their own privacy policies.

How information is used

  • To run and display the mechanics simulations.
  • To provide the optional Gemini physics assistant you request.
  • To protect the chat service and enforce rate limits.
  • To maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the website.

Sharing and retention

Mechsim does not sell personal information. Chat requests are shared with Google Gemini because that is the service you choose when using the assistant, and requests pass through Cloudflare because it hosts the proxy. The Mechsim Worker does not intentionally store OAuth tokens or chat histories. The browser keeps the current chat history only in page memory, so it is cleared when the page is closed or reloaded. Google, Cloudflare, and GitHub may retain information according to their own policies and terms.

Your choices

  • Use the simulations without connecting a Google account.
  • Do not send a question to Gemini if you do not want it processed.
  • Disconnect the Google account from the chat window at any time.
  • Revoke Mechsim’s Google access at Google Account permissions.

Children’s privacy

Mechsim is an educational project and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website or its data practices change. The date at the top of this page identifies the latest version.
